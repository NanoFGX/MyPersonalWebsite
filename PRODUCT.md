# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Vite + vanilla JavaScript + Three.js + GSAP (ScrollTrigger) + Lenis, static build deployed to GitHub Pages by a GitHub Actions workflow. Chosen by the owner from three offered options.

## Users

Recruiters, hiring managers and engineers screening internship candidates for DevOps, Cybersecurity (SOC) and Software Development roles. They arrive from a CV, LinkedIn or a GitHub link, usually on a laptop, and decide within a minute whether to read further or reach out. A secondary audience is technical interviewers who dig into projects and repositories.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio of Zakaria Bin Ali, final-year Computer Science student at Universiti Putra Malaysia (CGPA 3.84, PNB Merdeka Scholar), seeking a six-month internship from March 2027. Success: a visitor understands his three target roles, sees proof for each, and contacts him through LinkedIn or email.

## Positioning

A candidate who builds software, deploys it and defends it: live systems used by real people (311 registered users), competition-winning products, and hands-on SOC lab work with Wazuh and MITRE ATT&CK. The site itself is proof of craft: an interactive, scroll-driven WebGL experience shipped with a CI/CD pipeline.

## Operating Context

Visitors scan on desktop first, mobile second. Many will want a 30-second summary, so a recruiter quick view and printable summary exist alongside the full experience.

## Product Principles

1. Every claim maps to a line in the CV or a repository. No invented metrics.
2. Proof before adjectives: show the system, the number, the link.
3. Three roles, one story: build it, ship it, defend it.
4. The spectacle never blocks the content; the 30-second path is always one click away.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
