Scope: index.html, the single-page portfolio. Visitor mode: Experience (with a Persuade close).

Audience and job: internship recruiters for DevOps, SOC and software roles; decide fast, then contact via LinkedIn or email.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio is an operations console that is watching itself. It refuses the category default of a centered avatar hero, three equal skill cards and a timeline of logos; instead one live particle system re-forms into the subject of each section while real telemetry, logs and a working shell surround it.

OWN-WORLD: Blue-black instrument ground (#0B0D10 to #13171D), hairline panels at 8 to 16 percent opacity, off-white ink, one signal amber accent (#F5B03C) reserved for live state, primary action and selection. Geist for display and body, JetBrains Mono for telemetry, labels and the shell. Corners at 2px everywhere. Logos rendered monochrome and take their own brand colour only on hover.

STORY: Visitor learns who he is and the three roles in one viewport, sees hard numbers, then proof per role: stack, SOC lab console, projects with repos, experience, awards. Closes on a single contact moment.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left 6 of 12 columns: availability status line, name at display scale, 19-word subtext, primary "View work" and secondary "Recruiter view". Right half: particle globe, roughly 60 percent of viewport height, slowly rotating, reacting to the pointer. 64px nav on one line with Ctrl K trigger.

FORM: SOC Command Center, owner-pinned (concept roll skipped because the owner chose the direction). Seed key: owner-pinned.

Signature interaction: scroll-scrubbed staggered morph of 14k particles: globe, monogram, server racks, shield, helix, terrain, medal ring, lattice, portal. Motion grammar: ease-out entries at 200 to 600ms, no animation on keyboard-driven palette.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
